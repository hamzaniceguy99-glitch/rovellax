How we work

No shop, no commissions, no guesses about safety

Rovellax repairs nothing, inspects nothing and sells nothing. We coach owners, which means the advice can be that a job is worth paying for — something a shop is rarely positioned to say.

The safety line is firm: brakes, airbags and restraints, fuel systems, high-voltage hybrid components, steering, suspension and anything structural go to a qualified mechanic. Nothing over video can confirm a car is safe to drive, and we won't pretend otherwise.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Will you tell me what's wrong with my car?

We'll give you the most likely causes and how urgent it is, based on video, symptoms and codes. A diagnosis needs hands on the car, and we'll say when that's required.

Should I do my own brakes?

We don't coach brake work. It's the clearest example of a job where a mistake is catastrophic, and it belongs with a qualified mechanic.

Is the dealer's service list necessary?

Often partly. We'll go through it line by line against your manual and mileage, and it's common for half of it to be optional.

Can you inspect a car I want to buy?

Not physically. We'll prepare your checklist and questions, review photos and listings, and insist you get an independent pre-purchase inspection before paying.

Do you work on electric cars?

For ownership, tires, software and charging, yes. High-voltage systems are off limits entirely — that's specialist, dangerous work.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
