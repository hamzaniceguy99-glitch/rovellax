The programs, in detail

Four programs of 4 to 10 weeks, worked on your own vehicle, your mileage and your bills.

Program 01 · The jobs worth doing

Owner basics

We work through your own manual and build a real schedule for your car and mileage. Then the jobs most owners can do: checking and topping fluids, air filters, wipers, battery terminals, tire pressure and rotation — with clear rules about jack stands and what not to attempt.

  • 8 weeks
  • Complete beginners
  • 2 hours a week
  • Basic hand tools

Program 02 · Before you pay for guesses

Noises & warning lights

Send a video with sound and the conditions the noise appears in, and most symptoms narrow quickly. We cover reading codes with a cheap scanner, what common codes actually mean, and triage: drive it, book it, or stop driving now.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any owner
  • 2 hours a week
  • A $25 OBD reader

Start this program

Program 03 · Before the money moves

Buying a used car

We build a shortlist that matches your use and budget, then a walkaround checklist you can run in fifteen minutes, what the history report does and doesn't reveal, and why a pre-purchase inspection by an independent mechanic is the best hundred and fifty dollars in the process.

  • 6 weeks
  • Buying soon
  • 3 hours a week
  • Listings to review

Weeks 3–4 · Inspecting

Weeks 5–6 · Closing

Inspection, negotiation and paperwork.

Weeks 1–2 · Reading estimates

Parts, labor and the upsell list.

Program 04 · Stop overpaying

Estimates & shops

Bring an estimate and we'll go through it: what each line does, which items are genuinely due, where the labor time comes from, and what to ask. We also cover open recalls, warranty work and how to find a shop that treats you well.

  • 4 weeks
  • Any owner
  • 2 hours a week
  • A recent estimate
